STAR TRADER 0.2.3 — original space trading game for Omarchy

Install: bash install.sh
Play: open your app launcher and search for Star Trader.
Or run: python3 star_trader.py

1: complete flight manual. 2: version and update instructions.
3: fullscreen. ESC: quit. Update from a clean checkout: python3 update.py

Station: arrows select, B buy, S sell, U outfitting, F buy fuel,
R service hull/maintenance, P pay fines. L requests departure;
wait for clearance, then L launches. A low/insufficient-fuel warning
lets you buy fuel, cancel, or explicitly proceed. Buy and sell prices differ.
Cargo affects speed, turning, braking, fuel use and cruise time.

Flight: arrows steer, W accelerate, S brake/reverse, Q/E roll.
SPACE laser, V switch front laser, M missile, B deploy mine.
4 toggles front/rear view. Rear fire needs a purchased rear pulse laser.
C docking computer, Z jammer, K cloak (equipment must be bought).
D station, P planet, A asteroid/ore, J sun, O scoop nearby ore.
7 requests docking clearance. Regulated ports may refuse wanted pilots.
6 distress: nearby station rescue claims 25% cash AND cargo; outside
coverage, select/negotiate robot salvage offers before accepting.
8 engineering: train while docked; repair self/help disabled ships in flight.
Failure cooldowns start at 30 real minutes, doubling on consecutive failures.
Station service resets maintenance; neglect raises breakdown risk each flight.
Mouse: click rows, map locations, scanner contacts and action buttons.
Actions opens held movement/fire/hyperspace controls in the cockpit.

G galaxy map: / search planets, C coordinates, TAB free cursor,
arrows select/move, ENTER locks a waypoint. Hold H for 3 seconds: countdown, star-stream transit and arrival banner.
Hyperspace uses more fuel; release H before ignition to cancel. T starts slower, more economical cruise; T cancels.
Read the planet profile and budget fuel before departure.
Navigation uses gold dots/arrows. Click a scanner ship dot or press 5
to select a ship; its direction marker is pink. 4 reveals rearward targets.

Dock through the rotating slot at speed 1-25. Centre within
14m horizontal / 6m vertical; use Q/E to keep roll error under 9deg.
A docking computer is purchasable at sufficiently advanced stations.

Mining needs a mining laser and scoops. Break an asteroid with
SPACE, then approach ore fragments at speed <=50 and O to collect.
Illegal goods (*) risk confiscation and fines at regulated stations.

Station activities save automatically; quitting/dying in space
preserves the last station save. N after death replaces the save.
Save: ${XDG_DATA_HOME:-~/.local/share}/star-trader/save.json

Offline play works without Internet. Optional daily release checks
can be disabled with --no-update-check. Updates preserve saves.
Multiplayer, fleets, chat, player police and bases are future plans.
