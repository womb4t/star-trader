STAR TRADER — original Elite-inspired Python game

Run in a terminal:
  python3 star_trader.py

This laptop already has the required SDL2, SDL2_ttf and font libraries.
No pip packages or internet connection are needed.

At the station: Up/Down select goods, B buy, S sell, R refuel/repair,
L launch. Food is cheap on Lave and sells well on Zaonce.

In space: arrow keys steer, W accelerates, S slows down, SPACE fires.
G opens the galaxy chart; arrows select a system, H jumps.
D docks when the station is centred, under 350 metres away, speed
below 40 and no pirate is present. Fly towards the station with W;
start braking with S before reaching it. F1 opens the manual.
ESC exits. Saving happens at the station, including after trading.
Quitting or dying in space keeps your last station save.
N on the death screen starts a new pilot and replaces the save.

Saved progress: ~/.local/share/star-trader/save.json
Delete that file to start fresh.

This is a small tribute, not a port of the original Elite.
